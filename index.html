How it works

Four gentle steps. No signup drama. No pressure.

1

Pick how you're feeling

Open the app and choose what you need right now — someone to listen, emotional support, advice, or just a space to let it out.

2

Share what's on your mind

Write it out. Say it in a voice note. Stay completely anonymous — no name, no photo, no profile anyone can find.

3

People who get it respond

Not random comments. Warm, thoughtful responses from people who've been there — or who are there right now with you.

4

Feel a little lighter

That's the goal. Not a fix, not a solution — just the reminder that you're not alone in this.

Your safety isn't optional

We built the protection in before the features

🛡️

Kindness-first design

Every post has an intention — listen, support, or express — so responses match what you actually need.

🚨

Crisis support built in

If someone is in pain, we show them real helpline numbers — not a "contact us" form. Immediately.

🤝

No unsolicited advice

The community guidelines are clear: don't diagnose, don't fix, don't judge. Just be present.

🔒

Truly anonymous

No real names, no phone numbers, no location sharing. We literally can't see who you are.

👁️

Human moderation

Real people, not just AI. Every flagged post gets reviewed by someone who cares about this community.

🗑️

Your data, your call

Delete anything, anytime. No questions asked. Your story belongs to you — not to us.
